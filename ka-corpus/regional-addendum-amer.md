# AMER Regional Addendum (FY26)

> *AMER-specific rules layered on top of `coop-program-handbook.md` and `eligibility-matrix.md`. Where this addendum and the handbook conflict, **this addendum governs for AMER dealers** (US, Canada, Mexico).*

## 1. Scope

This addendum applies to all dealers in the AMER region: AMER East (US East + Atlantic Canada), AMER Central (US Central + Central Canada + Mexico), AMER West (US West + Pacific Canada).

## 2. Currency & tax

- All amounts in this region are denominated in **USD**. The portal does not perform conversion for AMER.
- Co-op funds are paid to the dealer as **marketing reimbursement**, not as a rebate. Dealers receive a Form 1099-MISC (US) or T4A (Canada) when annual payments exceed the local reporting threshold.
- Mexican dealers receive payments via wire in USD; the dealer is responsible for VAT/IVA treatment locally.

## 3. Hospitality activities (handbook §6.1, matrix §2.2.4)

US tax and ethics law require careful treatment of client hospitality. AMER-specific rules:
- The activity must have a **clear marketing purpose** (presentation, demonstration, or thought leadership). Pure entertainment is ineligible.
- Maximum per-attendee spend cap: **$185** (US) / **CAD 250** (Canada) for food and beverage.
- Public-sector attendees (federal, state, or municipal procurement officers) are **prohibited** from receiving hospitality benefits under this program. This is non-negotiable.
- Required additional documentation: the marketing-purpose statement and attendee-affiliation list (no PII beyond name and employer).

## 4. Website / Microsite (matrix §2.1.5) — AMER exception

AMER allows **Silver-tier and Authorized dealers** to claim against a **co-branded landing page** (not a full website) up to **$2,500** without escalating to Gold. The dealer must:
- Use the Steelcase Co-Branded Landing Page Template (linked in the dealer portal).
- Limit content to a single product family or campaign.
- Retire the landing page within 12 months of launch.

Full website builds remain restricted to Gold and Platinum per matrix §2.1.5.

## 5. NLA (Net Landed Allowance) interaction

In AMER, a separate NLA program offers price-adjustment funds to dealers for specific projects. **Co-op funds and NLA funds may not be applied to the same activity.** When a dealer is eligible for both:
- NLA = price-side support, applied to invoice.
- Co-op = marketing-side support, applied to claims.

The dealer must elect one on the project intake form. CMM and Regional Sales Director (RSD) coordinate to avoid double-application.

## 6. Dealer Council

The AMER Dealer Council convenes monthly (vs the global DPC quarterly cadence). The Council is the first stop for AMER-specific policy questions. Council representation rotates annually; current chair is the Dealer Council Chair appointed each March.

## 7. Trade-show calendar (AMER)

Pre-approval is **expedited (3 business days)** for the following AMER trade shows for FY26:
- NeoCon (Chicago, June)
- IIDA NEXT (rotating, late summer)
- Greenbuild (regional, fall)
- HD Expo (Las Vegas, spring)
- WorkSpring (Steelcase-hosted, March)

Trade-show participation outside this list follows the standard 5-business-day pre-approval SLA.

## 8. Reallocation rules (handbook §4)

AMER permits **inter-tier reallocation within a single region** with RMD approval — i.e., an unspent Gold-tier allocation can be temporarily reallocated to a Silver dealer demonstrating strong commitment for the second half. The reallocation does not change the dealer's tier or affect next-year base allocation.

## 9. Sustainability / Net-Zero claims

AMER follows the FTC Green Guides. Sustainability claims in dealer creative require:
- A **specific, quantified, substantiated** claim (no "eco-friendly", "green").
- Citation to the underlying certification (e.g., LEED, BIFMA Level, EPD) or third-party report.
- Avoidance of "Net-Zero" wording unless the activity directly supports the Steelcase Net-Zero Showroom Refresh program. Creative misuse of "Net-Zero" triggers `OFF_BRAND` rejection.

## 10. Dispute resolution venue

For AMER dealer disputes that escalate beyond the DPC, the resolution venue is **arbitration in Grand Rapids, MI** per the standard Steelcase Dealer Agreement (US) or in Toronto, ON (Canadian dealers).
