# EMEA Regional Addendum (FY26)

> *EMEA-specific rules layered on top of `coop-program-handbook.md` and `eligibility-matrix.md`. Where this addendum and the handbook conflict, **this addendum governs for EMEA dealers**.*

## 1. Scope

This addendum applies to dealers in EMEA North (UK, Ireland, Nordics, Benelux, Germany), EMEA South (France, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Greece), and EMEA East (Poland, Czechia, Hungary, Romania).

## 2. Currency

- The default reporting currency for EMEA is **EUR**.
- The portal converts requested amounts from local currency to EUR at the **ECB reference rate on the submission date**, with a ±2% tolerance band.
- For internal Steelcase reporting (and the gold layer of the analytics platform), values are also normalized to **USD** at the daily ECB EUR-USD rate.

## 3. GDPR

All marketing activities in EMEA must comply with **GDPR (EU)** and the **UK GDPR**:
- Lead capture at events: explicit opt-in is required; pre-ticked boxes are not consent.
- Email marketing: a documented lawful basis (consent for B2C, legitimate interest for B2B with opt-out).
- Pixel-based remarketing: prior consent management platform (CMP) integration with rejection-friendly defaults.
- Data residency: any dealer-collected lead data may not leave the EEA without an approved transfer mechanism (SCCs + TIA).

GDPR violations in dealer claim documentation are an automatic rejection (`OFF_BRAND` if creative, `DOC_INCOMPLETE` if proof artifact contains unconsented PII).

## 4. VAT

Co-op reimbursements paid by Steelcase to EMEA dealers are generally treated as **out-of-scope supplies** under the marketing-reimbursement classification. The dealer is responsible for local VAT treatment of any pass-through services (e.g., the dealer paying a vendor under a VAT-bearing invoice).

The portal does not produce a VAT invoice. Dealers requiring such documentation for local accounting should request a self-billing arrangement via their CMM.

## 5. Trade-show calendar (EMEA)

Pre-approval is **expedited (3 business days)** for the following EMEA shows in FY26:
- Orgatec (Cologne, October — biennial; major year FY26)
- 100% Design (London, September)
- Salone del Mobile / Workplace 3.0 (Milan, April)
- Stockholm Furniture & Light Fair (February)
- Maison & Objet (Paris, January and September)

Participation outside this list follows the standard 5-business-day pre-approval SLA.

## 6. Sustainability claims

EMEA, in particular the EU, has tightened rules on green claims (the EU Empowering Consumers Directive and the Green Claims Directive). EMEA-specific rules:

- **Generic terms banned**: "green", "eco-friendly", "ecological", "natural", "climate-friendly" cannot be used without **explicit, verifiable substantiation** in-creative or linked from-creative.
- **"Carbon-neutral" / "Net-Zero" / "carbon-positive"** require:
  - A published Greenhouse Gas Protocol-aligned methodology;
  - A third-party audit or certification reference;
  - The scope (1/2/3) and any offsets disclosed.
- **EU Ecolabel** logo and similar require evidence of certification on file.

EMEA misuse of sustainability vocabulary auto-fails the brand-compliance Voice & Messaging dimension and triggers `OFF_BRAND` rejection.

## 7. Language and localization

- Creative may be submitted in any EU/UK official language; the Creative Review Pod includes EN, FR, DE, ES, IT, PL reviewers.
- A single creative may be claimed once per language localization. A French version and a German version of the same campaign are two distinct claims, each requiring proof.
- Steelcase Voice translations are reviewed against the localized **Steelcase Brand Center** style guide (French, German, Spanish, Italian, Polish).

## 8. Showroom Refresh — historic-building considerations

EMEA showroom refresh activities frequently involve listed / heritage buildings (especially France, UK, Italy). The dealer must:
- Confirm any planning permission / heritage consent has been obtained.
- Avoid claiming for **structural** or **mechanical/electrical (M&E)** works — these are not eligible under any program.

## 9. Dealer Council (EMEA)

The EMEA Dealer Council meets quarterly, alternating between Munich and Amsterdam. Council seats are allocated 4 to EMEA North, 3 to EMEA South, 2 to EMEA East. Council elections occur in January for the upcoming fiscal year.

## 10. Reallocation rules (handbook §4)

EMEA permits **intra-region reallocation across sub-regions** with RMD approval. A reallocation from EMEA North to EMEA South is permissible mid-year; cross-region (EMEA → APAC, EMEA → AMER) is **not** permitted.
