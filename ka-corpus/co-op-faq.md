# Co-op Program FAQ (FY26)

> *Top 30 dealer questions with curated answers. Each answer points to the authoritative source document. If your question isn't here, search the handbook or ask your CMM.*

## Funding & Balance

**1. What is my current co-op balance?**
Open the **Co-op Dashboard** in the dealer portal, or ask COMPASS: *"What is my current balance?"* The balance shown is `allocated_usd - committed_usd`. See `coop-program-handbook.md §4`.

**2. When was my FY26 allocation communicated?**
By **February 15, 2026**, per the standard cadence. If you did not receive your allocation notification, contact your CMM. See `coop-program-handbook.md §3` and `§4`.

**3. How is my allocation calculated?**
75% tier-based + 25% performance-based. The full formula is in `coop-program-handbook.md §4`.

**4. Can I get a top-up mid-year?**
Yes, in narrow circumstances — strategic priority, regional shift, or RMD reallocation. Talk to your CMM. See `coop-program-handbook.md §4`.

**5. Can I roll unused funds into next year?**
No, with three narrow exceptions in `forfeiture-and-extension-policy.md §4`.

## Eligibility

**6. Is paid social eligible?**
Yes. Pre-approval required for activities ≥ $5,000. See `eligibility-matrix.md §2.1.2`.

**7. Is a competitor-comparison email eligible?**
No. Comparing competitors by name is prohibited per `brand-compliance-spec.md §6`.

**8. Can I claim for sponsoring my niece's school fundraiser?**
No. The activity must have a clear Steelcase brand-visible marketing component. See `eligibility-matrix.md §2.5.3`.

**9. Are SWAG and giveaways eligible?**
Limited. Branded SWAG distributed at an eligible event is part of the event claim; standalone SWAG distribution is not eligible.

**10. Is digital advertising for our dealership's recruiting eligible?**
No. The program funds **demand generation**, not recruiting.

**11. As a Silver-tier dealer, can I claim for a Hybrid Work Zone refresh?**
Yes. The Hybrid Work Zone is available to **all tiers** including Silver and Authorized. Pre-approval is required. See `eligibility-matrix.md §2.3.2`.

**12. As a Silver-tier dealer, can I claim for a generic showroom refresh?**
No. Generic Showroom Refresh (`§2.3.1`) is restricted to Gold and Platinum tiers. The Hybrid and Wellbeing zones are available to all tiers.

**13. As a Silver-tier dealer, can I claim for a website rebuild?**
No, with one exception: AMER Silver/Authorized dealers may claim a **co-branded landing page** up to $2,500 — see `regional-addendum-amer.md §4`. EMEA and APAC do not have this exception.

## Pre-approval

**14. When do I need pre-approval?**
For activities ≥ $5,000, all trade shows / NeoCon Regional, all showroom refreshes, and ABM Enterprise. Full list in `eligibility-matrix.md §2`. Process in `claim-submission-playbook.md §3`.

**15. How long does pre-approval take?**
5 business days standard; 3 business days for AMER expedited trade shows and EMEA expedited trade shows. See `regional-addendum-amer.md §7` and `regional-addendum-emea.md §5`.

**16. My pre-approval expired. Can I still claim?**
A pre-approval is valid for 90 days. If it expired and the activity has not been executed, file a new pre-approval. If executed before expiration, file the claim and reference the (now-expired) `preapproval_id` — it remains valid for the executed activity.

## Submitting a claim

**17. How do I submit a claim?**
Portal → **Claims > New Claim**, attach proof per `claim-submission-playbook.md §4`, submit.

**18. What proof do I need to upload?**
Depends on activity type — see the table in `claim-submission-playbook.md §4`.

**19. Why was my claim rejected with `MISSING_PREAPPROVAL`?**
The activity required pre-approval and the `preapproval_id` was not supplied or not on file. See `eligibility-matrix.md §2` for which activities require pre-approval and `claim-submission-playbook.md §3` for how to obtain one.

**20. Why was my claim rejected with `OFF_BRAND`?**
The creative scored below 50 on the brand-compliance rubric. The reviewer note lists the specific dimension(s) that failed. See `brand-compliance-spec.md §1`. You may resubmit with remediation; one re-review is included.

**21. Why was my claim rejected with `DOUBLE_DIP`?**
The same activity was claimed against two programs. The earlier claim is processed; the later is rejected. See `eligibility-matrix.md §5`.

**22. Why was my claim approved at a lower amount than I requested?**
The CMM determined that part of the activity was ineligible. The reviewer note must cite the eligibility-matrix section justifying the reduction. You may appeal — see `claim-submission-playbook.md §7.3`.

## Brand compliance

**23. Can I use a stock photo of an office?**
Maybe. The photo must adhere to the brand photo style (`brand-compliance-spec.md §5`). Stock photos from a banned-vendor list auto-fail.

**24. Can I use "Net-Zero" in my creative?**
Only if the activity supports the Steelcase Net-Zero Showroom Refresh program. In EMEA, the term is restricted further by EU green-claims rules — see `regional-addendum-emea.md §6`.

**25. Can I show competitor furniture in a comparison?**
No. Competitor names and products may not appear in dealer creative. See `brand-compliance-spec.md §6`.

## Money & payment

**26. When will I get paid?**
Target: 10 business days after the CMM's decision. Full timeline in `claim-submission-playbook.md §6`.

**27. How am I paid?**
ACH (AMER), bank transfer (EMEA, APAC). Currency: paid in your local currency at the spot rate on the decision date.

**28. Will I get a tax form?**
US dealers: Form 1099-MISC if annual payments exceed the threshold. Canadian dealers: T4A. EMEA: see `regional-addendum-emea.md §4` on VAT treatment.

## Forfeiture

**29. My balance is going to expire. What do I do?**
File submitted claims by **January 31** to commit funds. After that the funds are committed-but-not-yet-paid and remain protected. See `forfeiture-and-extension-policy.md §2`. If a true Steelcase-side or force majeure event blocked you, request an extension per `§4`.

**30. Can I appeal a forfeiture?**
A forfeiture is the result of unused allocation; there is no appeal of forfeiture itself. You may appeal a **rejected claim** that, if approved, would have prevented the forfeiture — see `claim-submission-playbook.md §7.3`.
