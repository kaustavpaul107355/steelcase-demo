# Steelcase Co-op Marketing Program Handbook (FY26)

> *This handbook is the authoritative reference for the Steelcase Co-op Marketing Program for fiscal year 2026 (March 1, 2026 – February 28, 2027). It supersedes all prior handbooks. Where a regional addendum (`regional-addendum-amer.md`, `regional-addendum-emea.md`) conflicts with this handbook, the regional addendum governs for that region.*

## 1. Program Intent

The Steelcase Co-op Marketing Program ("the Program") exists to **accelerate dealer-led demand generation** in the markets we serve. The Program funds marketing activities that:

1. Build brand presence in the dealer's local market.
2. Generate qualified pipeline for the dealer's sales team.
3. Deepen relationships with the architecture, design, and end-user communities.
4. Reinforce Steelcase's strategic narratives — Hybrid Work, Wellbeing, Learning, and Net-Zero.

The Program is **not** a discount mechanism, a price-support program, or a customer-rebate channel. Funds used to defray price, fulfill a contractual rebate, or pass through to an end customer are **ineligible** and will be recovered.

## 2. Program Governance

The Program is administered by the **Channel Marketing Office** in Grand Rapids, with regional execution by Channel Marketing Managers (CMMs) and Regional Marketing Directors (RMDs). Policy changes require approval by the **Dealer Program Council** (DPC), which convenes quarterly. Dealer representatives sit on the DPC by election.

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| Channel Marketing Office (CMO) | Owns program policy, eligibility matrix, and global budget. |
| Regional Marketing Director (RMD) | Owns the regional budget and dealer council. |
| Channel Marketing Manager (CMM) | Reviews and approves dealer claims; owns dealer success. |
| Finance Controller (Channel) | Owns accruals, payments, and audit. |
| Dealer Program Council (DPC) | Reviews policy changes; meets quarterly. |

## 4. Allocation Methodology

Annual allocations are computed in late January for the upcoming fiscal year and communicated to dealers by February 15.

**Tier-based component (75% of total allocation):**

| Tier | Allocation formula (USD) |
|------|---------------------------|
| Platinum | base $200,000 + 1.5% of prior-year sell-in |
| Gold | base $80,000 + 1.0% of prior-year sell-in |
| Silver | base $25,000 + 0.5% of prior-year sell-in |
| Authorized | base $8,000 |

**Performance component (25% of total allocation):**

Layered on top of the tier base, the performance component rewards dealers who:
- Met or exceeded their prior-year sell-through target (multiplier × 1.10)
- Achieved a brand-compliance score ≥ 85 on their prior-year claims (× 1.05)
- Submitted ≥ 75% of claims with first-pass approval (× 1.05)
- Participated in at least one **Net-Zero Showroom Refresh** initiative (× 1.10)

Multipliers are multiplicative, capped at × 1.25 in aggregate.

**Top-ups and reallocations:**
- The CMO may top up an allocation mid-year for strategic reasons.
- The RMD may **reallocate** unspent funds from a forecasted-underspending dealer to a forecasted-overspending dealer within their region, provided both dealers are at the same tier (or with explicit DPC waiver).

## 5. Eligible Activities (high-level)

The full eligibility matrix lives in `eligibility-matrix.md`. The five high-level activity categories are:

1. **Digital** — paid social, programmatic display, SEM, email, websites, account-based marketing, content syndication.
2. **Event** — trade shows, regional conferences (NeoCon Regional, IIDA, AIA), local dealer events, hospitality.
3. **Showroom** — physical refresh of dealer showroom, including the dedicated Hybrid Work, Wellbeing, and Net-Zero zones.
4. **Content** — sales literature, video, photography, case studies, whitepapers and ebooks.
5. **Sponsorship** — IIDA chapter, AIA chapter, community / civic.

## 6. Pre-approval

### 6.1 When pre-approval is required

Pre-approval is required for:
- Any single activity above **$5,000** (USD-equivalent at regional spot rate).
- Any **trade show** or **NeoCon Regional** event, regardless of amount.
- Any **showroom refresh** activity, regardless of amount.
- Any **ABM (Enterprise)** activity, regardless of amount.

Pre-approval is **not** required for:
- Email marketing
- Sales literature (reprints, fact sheets)
- Case studies
- Whitepapers / ebooks
- Single-spend social or SEM activities under $5,000

### 6.2 How to obtain pre-approval

Dealers submit a pre-approval request via the dealer portal. The CMM has **5 business days** to respond. A pre-approval, once issued, is valid for **90 days**. The dealer must include the `preapproval_id` on any subsequent claim against the pre-approved activity.

### 6.3 What happens if a dealer submits without required pre-approval

The claim will be moved to status `Rejected` with reason `MISSING_PREAPPROVAL`. The dealer may resubmit with a valid pre-approval if the activity has not yet been executed. The reviewer's note must cite **§6.1** of this handbook.

## 7. Co-fund Percentages

Steelcase funds a portion of each eligible activity; the dealer funds the remainder. Default co-fund percentages by activity type are listed in `eligibility-matrix.md §2`. Where a regional addendum or program-specific rule sets a higher dealer co-fund, the more dealer-favorable rate **does not** apply automatically; the dealer must request it in writing from their CMM.

## 8. Claim Submission

The end-to-end claim process — required documents, deadlines, escalation — is documented in `claim-submission-playbook.md`. Brand-compliance specs for creative review are in `brand-compliance-spec.md`.

## 9. Brand Compliance

Every claim involving creative (digital, video, photography, showroom signage) is subject to a brand-compliance score from 0–100. Scores below 70 trigger a mandatory remediation cycle; scores below 50 trigger rejection. See `brand-compliance-spec.md` for the rubric.

## 10. Audit & Anti-Fraud

Steelcase reserves the right to audit any claim. Common audit triggers:
- Unusual claim density (single-week spike of >20 claims from one dealer)
- Missing or inconsistent proof-of-execution
- Activity dates that fall outside the dealer's region
- Pre-approval ID reuse across multiple claims

Confirmed fraud results in: (a) recovery of paid funds, (b) freezing of remaining allocation, (c) referral to the DPC, and (d) for repeat offenders, removal from the Program for the following fiscal year.

## 11. Glossary

- **CMM** — Channel Marketing Manager (Steelcase employee, regional).
- **RMD** — Regional Marketing Director.
- **CMO** — Channel Marketing Office (global).
- **DPC** — Dealer Program Council.
- **Pre-approval** — required for activities above the threshold; protects dealer and Steelcase.
- **Brand compliance score** — 0–100 internal score from creative review.
- **Forfeiture** — unused allocation at FY-end (March 1 of the following year).
- **Sell-in** — sales from Steelcase to dealer; basis for allocation formula.
- **Sell-through** — sales from dealer to end customer.
