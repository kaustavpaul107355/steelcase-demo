# Brand Compliance Specification (FY26)

> *The rubric used by the Creative Review Pod to score every claim's creative work. Scores below 50 trigger rejection; 50–69 trigger remediation; 70+ proceeds. This document is the rubric, not the brand guidelines themselves — see the **Steelcase Brand Center** for assets and full guidelines.*

## 1. Scoring rubric (100 points)

Each submission is scored on five dimensions:

| Dimension | Points | What we look for |
|-----------|--------|------------------|
| Logo treatment | 20 | Correct logo, clearspace, sizing, placement, color treatment |
| Color palette | 20 | Approved palette only; no off-brand color tints |
| Typography | 15 | Approved fonts; correct weights and tracking |
| Photography & imagery | 20 | On-brand photo style; no stock photos that violate guidelines |
| Voice & messaging | 25 | On-brand voice; correct strategic narrative; no prohibited claims |

A score under 50 in any single dimension also caps the overall to ≤ 49 (auto-rejection).

## 2. Logo treatment (20)

- The **Steelcase logo** must appear in approved color (Steelcase Red `#E5172F`, white reverse, or black) on a non-clashing background.
- Minimum clearspace = 1× the height of the "S" in the logo on all sides.
- Minimum logo size: 100px tall in digital; 0.5 inches in print.
- The logo must not be stretched, recolored, outlined, or used as a decorative element.
- Dealer logo and Steelcase logo must be co-presented with **equal visual weight** — neither dominates.

Common deductions:
- Logo touches edge of canvas (-5)
- Logo over busy photograph (-5)
- Recolored or stylized logo (auto-fail dimension)

## 3. Color palette (20)

Approved primary palette:
- Steelcase Red `#E5172F`
- Surface White `#F4F4F2`
- Charcoal `#2C2C2C`
- Slate `#5A6B72`

Approved accent palette (use sparingly):
- Lichen `#7E8C5C`, Pollen `#E2BE5C`, Cyan `#3FB1C5`, Plum `#5A2F62`

Tints below 70% are not approved without prior brand-team waiver.

Common deductions:
- Off-palette gradient (-5)
- Competitor-adjacent colors used prominently (-5)
- Use of black for body text instead of Charcoal (-2)

## 4. Typography (15)

Approved fonts: **Söhne**, **Söhne Mono**, **Recoleta** (display only).

- Body text: Söhne Regular, weight 400, tracking 0.
- Headlines: Söhne Buch or Söhne Halbfett; or Recoleta Medium for display.
- Avoid all-caps for body copy.
- Never substitute Helvetica, Arial, or any generic sans-serif.

Common deductions:
- Substitute font in body (-8)
- All-caps in long body copy (-3)

## 5. Photography & imagery (20)

- **Photo style**: natural light, authentic moments, real workplaces. Avoid stock-photo tropes (handshakes, cliché office settings).
- **Talent**: diverse representation, age and ability inclusive, no exclusively-young-knowledge-worker depictions.
- **Furniture**: Steelcase products in correct context. Do **not** photoshop competitor products in.
- **Settings**: real or styled workplaces; avoid white-cyc product-shot style except for catalog imagery.

Auto-fail triggers:
- Stock photo from a banned vendor list (see Brand Center, refreshed quarterly)
- Misrepresentation of product features in the image

## 6. Voice & messaging (25)

Steelcase brand voice is **considered, expert, hopeful**.

**Do:**
- Lead with people and the work they do.
- Reference our strategic narratives — Hybrid Work, Wellbeing, Learning, Net-Zero — where relevant.
- Cite research (Steelcase Workplace Survey, etc.) accurately and with attribution.

**Don't:**
- Make superlative claims that aren't verifiable ("the world's best", "#1").
- Make environmental or sustainability claims without citing the underlying certification or report. **Net-Zero, carbon-neutral, sustainable, circular** are regulated terms in EMEA — see `regional-addendum-emea.md §6`.
- Make medical or wellbeing health-outcome claims ("reduces back pain by 30%") without published clinical evidence.
- Reference competitors by name.

Auto-fail triggers:
- Unsubstantiated sustainability claim
- Medical-outcome claim without evidence
- Competitor name in copy

## 7. Score-to-decision mapping

| Score | Result |
|-------|--------|
| 0–49 | Auto-rejected. Dealer may resubmit with corrections. |
| 50–69 | **Remediation required.** Reviewer provides specific edits; one re-review is included. |
| 70–84 | **Approved with notes.** Reviewer surfaces minor issues for the dealer's next cycle. |
| 85–100 | **Approved.** Counts toward the dealer's performance multiplier for next FY (see handbook §4). |

## 8. Common rejection patterns by category

| Category | Top three reasons for sub-50 scores |
|----------|-------------------------------------|
| Digital — Programmatic Display | Font substitution; off-palette gradient; logo too small |
| Digital — Paid Social | Stock-photo trope; competitor-adjacent color; all-caps copy |
| Showroom Refresh | Wrong signage typography; logo clearspace violation; off-brand wayfinding color |
| Event — Trade Show | Booth wayfinding off-palette; printed handouts use Helvetica |
| Content — Video | Voiceover tone off-brand; cliché B-roll; mis-cited research |

## 9. Re-review process

A claim moved to **Remediation** has 15 business days for the dealer to upload revised creative. The Creative Review Pod re-reviews within 3 business days. Only **one** re-review is included in the standard SLA; a second re-review may be requested but extends the cycle by 5 business days.

## 10. Appeal

Dealers may appeal a score to the **Director of Creative Strategy**. The director has 10 business days to respond and may adjust the score, sustain it, or refer to the DPC.
